How a job runs

From scope to close-out

The same sequence on every project, whether it is a single valve or a full rooftop replacement. Owners know what is happening and when.

  1. Step 01

    Walkthrough and scope

    We look at the equipment, the mechanical room, and the access before anything is priced. Existing conditions get photographed and noted.

  2. Step 02

    Proposal and plan

    A written scope with the equipment, the crane and rigging plan if one is needed, the shutdown windows, and how occupied areas stay protected.

  3. Step 03

    Install

    Our own licensed journeymen do the work: crane sets, trench piping, welded stainless duct, containment in corridors, and the small details in between.

  4. Step 04

    Test and hand back

    Systems are started, checked, labeled, and insulated. Walls and ceilings are closed up and the space is handed back clean.
